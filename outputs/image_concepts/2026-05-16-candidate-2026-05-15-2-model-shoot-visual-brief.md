## Concept Title
Archive-worn editorial

## Use Case
Realistic model shoot for IG/TikTok feed + future photoshoot reference

## Image Prompt
Editorial fashion photo of a model wearing the **Distressed Grommet Eyelet Tank Top**, styled as a sharp, minimal archive fragment made wearable; close-fitting tank, distressed eyelet/grommet texture clearly visible, natural skin texture, premium studio lighting, dark neutral backdrop, slightly raw but polished fashion mood, clean product-first composition, full-body and mid-shot crop options, high realism, contemporary street-luxe styling

## Negative Prompt
No extra garments not in source, no invented logos, no fantasy fabric, no CGI look, no over-stylized makeup, no busy set, no excessive whitespace, no poster layout, no text blocks in frame, no unrealistic anatomy, no glossy plastic skin

## Styling Notes
Black or charcoal bottom, bare arms, understated jewelry, slick hair or soft natural texture, editorial but believable stance, center-weighted framing, garment must remain the focus

## Feed Role
Product clarity with a lived-in fashion proof point

### Concept Title
Tight Crop / Hard Light

### Use Case
AI model-shoot direction for a product clarity feed post.

### Image Prompt
Realistic editorial fashion photo of a female model wearing the distressed grommet eyelet tank top, styled minimal and monochrome to keep the garment dominant. Tight crop from collarbone to waist, clean front-facing pose, subtle tension in the fabric, strong visibility of eyelets and distressing. Dark neutral studio backdrop, hard side light, crisp shadow edges, premium magazine finish, high contrast, no extra props, garment reads as the object.

### Negative Prompt
Illustration, painterly look, extra garments, loud accessories, busy background, text, watermark, overstyled makeup, exaggerated pose, fantasy styling, distorted anatomy, opaque fabric hiding details.

### Styling Notes
- Keep the tank top as the focal point.
- Use neutral bottoms only if visible.
- Minimal jewelry, clean skin, slick hair.
- Frame for fabric texture + hardware clarity.
- Avoid white space in composition; fill frame.

### Feed Role
Hero product image; commercial anchor after the origin-story content.

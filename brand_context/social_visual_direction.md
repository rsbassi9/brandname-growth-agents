# Instagram Visual Direction Reference

These screenshots are reference context for the agents, not a fixed standard to copy. The current page shows the brand before the dashboard-led system was introduced: useful signals, uneven direction.

## Current Signals
- Dark interface context, high contrast black/white brand language, type-led archive labels, and bracketed `[BRAND NAME.]` marks.
- Strongest posts combine real wearability with art reconstruction: models wearing garments, back graphics, close product crops, and source-painting fragments.
- The grid already contains a mix of product mockups, campaign photos, process/design files, art details, and casual lifestyle proof.
- Posts with body/product clarity are important because the brand can otherwise become too abstract.
- Scribbled annotation, scan/archive marks, white line overlays, and label-like text can work when restrained.

## Problems To Improve
- The current grid has inconsistent spacing, type scale, and content rhythm.
- Some product posts are too mockup-heavy; some abstract posts do not explain the transformation quickly enough.
- The feed needs clearer sequencing: art/source, reconstruction/process, then wearable/product proof.
- Keep the experimental visual language, but make the output feel more intentional, premium, and repeatable.

## Direction For New Posts
- Treat the brand as fine art reconstructed into wearable objects.
- Use real store/product shots as the garment source of truth.
- Use campaign and body shots to prove wearability every few posts.
- Use process/design assets to create credibility and brand world, not filler.
- Make hooks direct and legible in the first frame, especially for Reels/TikTok.
- Prefer quiet premium layouts with severe spacing, not busy collage unless the concept demands it.
- Let the agents improve the aesthetic over time through feedback and performance, rather than preserving the current grid exactly.

## Dashboard Implication
- Feed curation should show whether a proposed post supports product clarity, transformation narrative, process evidence, or brand meaning.
- Calendar ordering should avoid stacking too many abstract/process posts in a row.
- The Post Builder should make source assets, caption, hook, structure, and status visible together before approval.
